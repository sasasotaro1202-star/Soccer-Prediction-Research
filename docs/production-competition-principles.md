# Production competition principles

1. Cover as many major leagues, domestic cups and European competitions as practical.
2. Keep every competition's provenance and verification state explicit.
3. Do not let coverage expansion lower the production gate.
4. Unknown PIT evidence remains unknown.
5. No competition enters production without auditable pre-kickoff availability evidence and chronological OOS validation.
6. 1X2, Score and MOM have independent eligibility and abstention gates.
7. Real-world robustness and calibration outrank historical backtest score.
8. Changes should be reproducible, reviewable and safe to rerun.
